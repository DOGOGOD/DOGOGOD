## A Little About Me

Hi, I'm **Guztchian**. I drift between technology and literature — describing the world with mathematics, reshaping it with code, and, now and then, trying to understand it with words.

A Software Engineering student at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, I'm drawn to AI, agents, and robotics — to the slow, stubborn craft of teaching machines to perceive, reason, and act. I'm equally captivated by mathematics, physics, and space, and by the quiet audacity of trying to understand the universe.

My [personal site](https://dogogod.github.io) is a notebook and a quiet place to land, where technical reflections, project notes, and small moments worth keeping find their way onto the page. If any of this resonates, you're always welcome there.

## Selected Work

<sub>A small selection of things I have enjoyed thinking through, building, and refining.</sub>

### 01 · [SJTUClaw](https://github.com/DOGOGOD/SJTUClaw)

**A local AI agent runtime for personal and educational workflows.** It brings multi-turn conversations, tool calling, long-term memory, Skills, and scheduled tasks into one system, with desktop, Web UI, TUI, CLI, REST API, and QQ Bot interfaces.

<sub>AI agents · Local-first · Tool use · Memory · Multi-interface</sub>

### 02 · [Office Preview](https://github.com/DOGOGOD/Office-Preview)

**An Obsidian plugin for reading Office files without leaving your notes.** Word, PowerPoint, and Excel previews support zoom, one-click text copy, multi-sheet navigation, automatic file routing, light and dark themes, and responsive desktop and mobile layouts.

<sub>TypeScript · Obsidian API · DOCX · PPTX · XLSX</sub>

### 03 · [WeRead](https://github.com/DOGOGOD/WeRead)

**A scheduled reading pipeline for WeChat Official Accounts.** It collects recent articles, filters them around personal interests, extracts the useful content with Playwright, and turns it into concise AI reading briefs.

<sub>Python · Playwright · Scheduling · AI summarization</sub>

### 04 · [MineSweeper](https://github.com/DOGOGOD/MineSweeper)

**A cross-platform C++ Minesweeper made for the console.** Alongside mouse input and colorful rendering, it includes flood-fill expansion, chord clearing, flags, first-click protection, custom grids, multiple difficulty levels, a timer, and sound effects.

<sub>C++ · Console graphics · Mouse input · Cross-platform</sub>
